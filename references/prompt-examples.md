# Prompt Examples

These examples demonstrate how to separate scene structure, style, and information density. Replace bracketed text with the user's actual scene details.

## Restyle a photograph

```text
Restyle the supplied photograph as an anime game environment background.
Use the photograph as the scene and composition source: preserve [building masses, walkway, horizon, and spatial relationships].
Use the supplied anime environment image only as a rendering and palette reference; do not copy its location or composition.
Use a calm eye-level medium/long view, Balanced information density, grouped architectural forms, selective linework, and restrained surface detail.
Keep the environment unoccupied. No character staging, close foreground framing, dramatic camera, or imposed story mood.
```

## Reduce scene detail

```text
Keep the same location, camera height, and major spatial relationships. Simplify this scene to Sparse information density: retain the main silhouettes and distinctive landmarks, merge repetitive small details into broad shapes, and keep the environment readable as an anime game background.
```

## Increase environmental detail

```text
Preserve the existing eye-level establishing composition and add Rich environmental detail through a few secondary fixtures, material cues, and local variations. Keep detail selective and distributed; do not introduce characters, foreground framing, dramatic lighting, or a new narrative focal point.
```

## Change time or weather only

```text
Keep the architecture, layout, camera, and information density unchanged. Change only the requested time/weather to [condition], using restrained, plausible light and atmosphere. Do not add people, props, or story cues.
```
